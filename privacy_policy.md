# Privacy Policy for Röst

_Last updated: 2026 01 24_

Röst (“the App”) is provided by Ragnar Development (“we,” “our,” or “us”) as a mobile application available on the Apple App Store. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Röst.

---

## Information We Collect
Röst itself does not require you to create an account with us, and we do not collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information.  

However, when you use the App, you may provide connection details to a third-party or self-hosted audiobook server (for example, a server address, username, or access token). This information is:

- **Stored locally** on your device to allow the App to connect to your chosen server.  
- **Transmitted only** to the server you configure in the App.  
- **Not shared** with us or with any other third party.  

Additionally:

- **App Data**: Preferences and playback information are stored locally on your device.  
- **Diagnostics**: Apple and your device may collect anonymized crash reports or analytics if you have opted in via system settings. We do not have access to personally identifiable information in these reports.

---

## How We Use Information
We use the information you provide only to:

- Connect to the audiobook server you select.  
- Enable playback, browsing, and other features of the App.  
- Maintain local preferences such as playback position.  

We do **not** use your information for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

---

## Third-Party or Self-Hosted Servers
Röst acts as a client to servers that are operated by third parties or self-hosted by you. Please note:

- We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy or security practices of the servers you choose to connect to.  
- The data available to those servers (such as your account information, usage data, or media library) is governed by the policies of the server operator.  
- If you are connecting to a server hosted by someone else, you should review their privacy practices.  

---

## Third-Party Services
Röst does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.  
The App does rely on Apple’s operating system and App Store infrastructure, which may collect limited usage data in accordance with [Apple’s privacy policies](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

---
